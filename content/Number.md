# Overview
Numbers are mathematical objects used as a representation of quantity, position or value. 

# Number Sets
## Natural Numbers
These are the fundamental set of numbers, typically starting at $1$ and continuing indefinitely. These numbers are mostly used for counting objects.

$$\huge \{1,2,3,…\}$$
## Integers
Extend [natural numbers](Number.md#Natural%20Numbers) by including their negative counterpart and $0$. These numbers are used to express quantities with deficits (e.g., accounting, temperature).

$$\{ \huge …, −2, −1, 0, 1, 2, …\}$$
## Rational Numbers
Rational numbers $\huge\mathbb{Q}$ are any number that can be written as a [fraction] where the numerator and denominator are [integers](Number.md#Integers).  Rational numbers include terminating and repeating [[Decimal (Base 10)]].

## Real Numbers
Real numbers form the complete [set](Set%20Theory.md) of all numbers that can be placed on a one dimensional number line. This set includes all [rational](Number.md#Rational%20Numbers) and all [irrational numbers](Number.md#Irrational%20Numbers).
