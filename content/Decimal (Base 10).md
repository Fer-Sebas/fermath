## Overview
Decimals are a numeric system based on powers of 10. It has 10 characters to represent quantities (0-9). It's the most common used numeric system by humans.

#### Steps to divide decimals with fractional parts

1. **Turn the divisor into a whole number**:
   If the divisor has decimals, multiply both the **divisor** and the **dividend** by $10^n$, where $n$ is the number of decimal places in the divisor, to make the divisor a whole number. 
   

> [!EXAMPLE]
> **Problem:** $18\div0.6$  
> 
>  The divisor ($0.6$) has 1 decimal place. Multiply both the **divisor** and the **dividend** by $10$ to make the divisor a whole number:  
> $$18 \div 0.6 = \frac{0.6×10}{18×10}​= \frac{180}{6}$$
>    

1. **Perform the Division**:
   Divide the numbers as if they were whole numbers.
   
2. **Adjust the Decimal Point in the Result**:
   The position of the decimal in the dividend determines the placement of the decimal in the result.
