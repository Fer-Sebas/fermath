A function is a relationship between two [sets](Set%20Theory.md) called **domain** (source set) and **codomain** (target set) where a well-defined rule, assigns exactly one element (output) in the codomain to each element (input) in the domain.

$$\huge f: A \rightarrow B ~~or~~ f: A \rightarrow A$$

A function is often denoted by a letter such as $f$, $g$ or $h$.

$$\Huge f(x)=x+a, ~ and ~ if ~ a=2,~then ~ f(2)=2+2=4$$
