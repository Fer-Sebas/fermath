Number theory is the study of [numbers](Number.md), their properties and the manipulation of these numbers using [operations](Operation.md).
## Numbers
Numbers are abstract objects used to count, measure, and label. Numbers form the foundation of number theory and by extension, all of mathematics.

Numbers are visually represented using symbols called numerals.

Numbers are organized into sets ($N$, $Z$, $Q$) based on their properties. Numbers can be expressed in different ways called systems (binary, decimal, hexadecimal).

## Fundamental operations
The fundamental operations are: Subtraction and multiplication along with their opposites, subtraction and division.
