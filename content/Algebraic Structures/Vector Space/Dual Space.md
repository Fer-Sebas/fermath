# Overview
A dual space $V^*$ of a [[Vector Space]] $V$ over a field $k$ is the set of all [linear functionals](Linear%20Form.md) from $V$ to $k$. 

$$\huge V^* = \{f: V \to k \space | \space is \space linear\} $$

$V^*$ is itself a vector space with respect to the natural pointwise operations.
