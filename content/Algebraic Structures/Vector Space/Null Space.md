# Overview
The null space of a [linear transformation](Linear%20Transformation) from one vector space to another, is the **subset of the first vector space** consisting of **all vectors that the transformation maps to zero**.
<br><br>
$$\Large null(T) = \{\space v \in V \space |\space T(v) = 0\space \}$$
<br><br>
The null space captures **what gets collapsed to nothing** by the transformation.

It lives entirely in the **domain**, not the codomain.
