**Relationship**: 
A general, unconstrained connection or link between elements of two sets.

**Mapping**: 
A precise, rule-based assignment of the elements from one set to another. One-to-one and deterministic.
Synonym with: *Function, Transformation.*

**Association**: 
Any pairing between of elements of two sets.

**Sum**:
The result from addition.

**Difference**:
The result from a subtraction.

**Term**:

**Multiple**:
A multiple is the result of multiplying any integer by another.

**Product**: 
The result from a multiplication.

**Factor**:
A factor is any integer that divides another integer without leaving a remainder.

**Quotient**: 
The whole number result from a division.

**Remainder**:
The amount left over after division when one number does not divide another exactly.
